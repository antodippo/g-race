Kerbside GP

Ten small cars, five teams, one screen. Pick a circuit, pick a team, and beat the other nine to the flag.

Circuit

Championship: click circuits to add or remove them from the season (highlighted ones are in). Rounds run in the order shown. With qualifying on, you run three timed laps alone on track before each race and the fastest lap sets the grid; with it off the grid is random. Points 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1, plus 1 for the fastest lap of each race.

Your team — you drive the plain car, your team-mate's has white wings and a white helmet

Changes apply to both cars in the team and are saved in this browser.

↑ W throttle · ↓ S brake / reverse · ← → A D steer · Space pause · F full screen · R restart · Esc back to garage. Grass and sand slow you down, and a wet track has less grip. A pit stop fits wet tyres (blue wheels) if the track is wet and slicks if it is dry; each is slow in the other's conditions. Tuck in behind another car on a straight for a slipstream. Fuel and tyres last about six laps: to refuel, fit new tyres and repair damage, stop in the pit strip beside the start straight and wait. Contact with other cars damages and slows both.
